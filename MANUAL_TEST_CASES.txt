=====================================================================
Priority: High      Type: End-to-end      Preconditions: not logged in, empty cart
Test data: product Espresso Beans 250g, promo BREW10, the test card above

 #  Step                                                     Expected result
 1  Open the site.                                           Home page loads. Header shows Cart 0.
 2  On "Espresso Beans 250g" click "Add to cart".            Toast "Added Espresso Beans 250g to cart". Cart badge shows 1.
 3  Click the Cart button in the header.                     Cart drawer opens. Line shows $12.50 each.
 4  Type BREW10 in the promo field and click Apply.          Message "Code BREW10 applied: 10% off".
 5  Read the totals.                                         Subtotal 12.50, Discount -1.25, Shipping 4.50, Total 15.75.
 6  Click Checkout.                                          Checkout page, step "1. Shipping" highlighted.
 7  Click Continue with every field empty.                   Error "Please fill in all shipping fields."
 8  Enter name Jane Doe, email "bad", address, city; Continue. Error "Please enter a valid email."
 9  Fix the email to jane@example.com; click Continue.       Step "2. Payment" is highlighted.
10  Enter card number 123, other fields valid; Continue.     Error "Card number must be 16 digits."
11  Enter 4111111111111111; Continue.                        Step "3. Review" shows the item and Total 15.75.
12  Click Place order WITHOUT ticking the terms box.         Error "Please accept the terms and conditions."
13  Tick the terms box; click Place order.                   "Thank you for your order!", an order number BC-xxxxxx,
                                                              and a summary with total $15.75 and jane@example.com.
